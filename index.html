How it works

Both directions

You hear the other side in your language, with translated voice and captions. They hear your translated voice as your microphone.

Straight to the engine

Audio goes from your browser to OpenAI or Google Gemini Live with your own API key. Nothing passes through our servers.

2–3 seconds behind

Translation follows the speaker with a delay of about two to three seconds, in any Google Meet call.
