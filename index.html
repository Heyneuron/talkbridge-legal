Talkbridge — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Talkbridge is a browser extension made by BIG GROWTH Sp. z o.o. (HeyNeuron, heyneuron.com). It translates your Google Meet calls live.

What the extension processes

  • Call audio: your microphone and the audio of other participants, while translation is running.
  • Captions and transcripts: the text of the translation.
  • Settings: languages, options, your API keys and license key.
  • Usage counter: minutes and estimated cost.

Where it goes

Audio is sent straight from your browser to the translation engine you choose, using your own API key:

  • OpenAI (api.openai.com), under the OpenAI privacy policy, or
  • Google Gemini API (generativelanguage.googleapis.com), under the Gemini API terms.

The license key is checked with Gumroad (api.gumroad.com). Gumroad receives only the key.

We run no servers for this extension. We do not receive, store or sell your audio, transcripts, keys or any personal data. There is no analytics or tracking.

Stored on your device

Settings, keys, the usage counter and the last 20 transcripts are kept in the extension's local storage on your computer. You can delete transcripts in the settings. Uninstalling the extension removes everything.

Your responsibility

Recording and translating other people's speech may require their consent where you live. Tell the other participants that you are using live translation.
